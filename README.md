Economics Research Archive & Seed Ideas
📚 Vision & Philosophy
Human knowledge is a continuous flow. This project was created with profound humility in the face of the infinity of time, space, and human history. This repository serves as a personal archive where I aggregate my economic datasets, research summaries, and unfinished "seed ideas."

The purpose of this project is not to provide absolute conclusions, but to lay down foundational bricks. It is my sincere hope that future generations of researchers can access, modify, perfect, and continue the journey that I could not complete within the limits of a single lifetime.

"A single lifetime is small, but shared knowledge is eternal."

⚖️ Repository Contents
📜 01_Research_Summaries: Contains concise summaries of the context, methodology, and core lessons learned from my past economic research projects.

🔬 02_Open_Data: Cleaned and structured economic datasets, shared to help future researchers bypass the tedious data collection phase.

💡 03_Seed_Ideas: Open questions, untested hypotheses, and potential research directions that are waiting to be explored.

⚙️ 04_Code_and_Models: Scripts for data analysis and econometric modeling.

🤝 Open to the Future
This project is entirely open source. You are completely free to download, use, critique, and expand upon any content found here. I invite you to step forward and complete what I have left open.
